# DocScan AI 0.3.16

The first build where the detector is **primarily AI-driven** instead of falling back to the
classical path, and the first build whose input pipeline no longer destroys the model's accuracy.

> **Correction (2026-11-15).** An earlier version of these notes said "correct 63 → 84". That
> number came from the gate's `correct` label, which only means *detected and not flagged* — it
> never meant the quad was the page. Measured properly against the annotated page, **44 of 95
> scoreable detections (46 %) have IoU ≥ 0.5**, up from 40 of 88 (45 %) in 0.3.15. Mean IoU rose
> 0.459 → 0.480. The notes below, and `docs/runs/detector-116.md`, now use the strict metric.
> Nine photos turned out to have unusable annotations and are excluded (details below).

## Headline numbers

Measured on 116 real owner photos through the real in-app detector
([`docs/runs/detector-116.md`](https://github.com/kuzeyt32/DocScanAI/blob/main/docs/runs/detector-116.md)):

| metric | 0.3.15 | **0.3.16** |
|---|---|---|
| **gate** | FAIL | **PASS** |
| detected | 97 | **104** |
| **CORNER_MODEL (AI)** | 33 | **79** |
| CLASSICAL_FALLBACK | 64 | **25** |
| no-detection | 19 | **12** |
| wrong-page | 13 | **11** |
| merged | 24 | **11** |
| **quad is the page (IoU ≥ 0.5)** | 40 / 88 (45 %) | **44 / 95 (46 %)** |
| mean IoU | 0.459 | **0.480** |
| unscoreable oracle annotations | 9 | 9 |

The AI path more than doubles and the classical fallback drops by two thirds. Strict quad quality
moves only slightly: **most of the remaining error is geometric, not detection**.

## What was actually wrong

### 1. The model was fed an aspect-squashed image (letterbox fix)

`Bitmap.createScaledBitmap(bitmap, 256, 256, true)` squashes a 4:3 phone still into a square. The
page arrived stretched 33 % vertically, and a corner regressor trained on undistorted pages returned
nothing. Now the input is letterboxed — aspect preserved, flat grey pad, cubic resampling — and a
heat peak landing on the pad is rejected rather than mapped to a fake corner.

| input construction | photos with a 4-corner lock | model quads matching the page |
|---|---|---|
| squash + bilinear (0.3.15) | 30 | 23 |
| squash + cubic | 33 | 25 |
| **letterbox + cubic (0.3.16)** | **61** | **42** |

(Model output only, host replay over 100 photos with a usable annotation. This is not the
end-to-end shipped accuracy — see the headline table for that.)

### 2. Small pages got lost among other sheets (multi-crop rescue)

When a page fills little of the frame, even the fixed input returns peaks of ~0.001. If the
full-frame pass fails **on a still/import**, the model now retries on 4 overlapping quadrants plus
a centre crop and takes the first plausible result.

| strategy | model quads matching the page |
|---|---|
| full frame only | 42 |
| **best of 6 crops** | **52** |

All 10 gained photos had *no* full-frame lock, so the retry is skipped for everything that already
works. It is still-only (~220 ms once per capture, too slow for 30 fps preview).

## Colour is not the cause

Measured with the paper colour taken *inside* the page, excluding the 9 unusable annotations:

| paper class | photos | missed | miss rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| white / neutral | 33 | 7 | 21 % |
| tinted | 65 | 3 | 5 % |
| green / coloured | 9 | 2 | 22 % |

White and coloured paper are missed at almost the same rate. Paper colour is **not** a driver of
detection failure in this corpus.

## The "box inside the page" report

The owner reported that 1-2 of the 4 corners land on a box corner *inside* the page. Corner-by-corner
measurement over 94 scorable photos ([`docs/runs/corner-signatures.md`](https://github.com/kuzeyt32/DocScanAI/blob/main/docs/runs/corner-signatures.md)):

| what the quad touches | photos |
|---|---|
| all 4 corners on the page | 19 |
| **all 4 corners on an inner box** | **1** |
| 1-2 corners on the page, the rest inside it | 7 |
| exactly 2 page corners + 2 box corners | 0 |
| quad overshoots the page | 34 |
| quad lands on a different region | 37 |

So the box-lock is real but rare in stills, and the one unambiguous case came from
**`CLASSICAL_FALLBACK`**, not the corner model. Three of the four "entirely inside" quads are one
page of an open spread or a loose sheet on a cover — arguably correct behaviour against an
annotation that marks the whole spread.

## Oracle annotation defects

Nine photos cannot be scored at all: five have all four oracle corners collapsed onto the frame
corner (never annotated), one has a 0.67 %-of-frame quad, three have a duplicated corner with a
zero-length side. Against a zero-area oracle, IoU is 0 by construction and `merged`
(`area_ratio >= 1.5`) is infinite, so **earlier reports carried fabricated wrong-page/merged flags**
on those rows. `wrong-page` and `merged` are now only computed on scoreable rows.

This also corrects the earlier claim of a single regression: that photo (`20261024_190237`) has a
collapsed annotation and cannot regress. On scoreable photos 0.3.16 gains 10 strict quad matches
and loses 6; three of the six losses are the single-page-of-a-spread cases above.

## Fixed along the way

- **Silent channel-mismatch bug (mine, caught before release):** `Utils.bitmapToMat` returns RGBA;
  copying into a 3-channel canvas fails *without throwing*, so the model was fed pure padding and
  CORNER_MODEL briefly dropped to zero. Preprocessing now normalises to BGR and asserts the
  composite is not empty.
- **`corner_input.png` in the trace dump** now shows the real letterboxed model input.
- **Model setup screen** offers only the two artifacts the pipeline reads (~35 MB instead of
  ~110 MB).
