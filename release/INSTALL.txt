DocScan AI 0.3.17 - what changed

Two page-detection fixes, both found on 42 brand-new phone photos that were never used
for tuning, and both verified against the 116-photo set the detector was originally
measured on.

1. BOTH PAGES OF AN OPEN BOOK ARE NOW ONE SCAN
   Before: the detector could lock a two-page spread and then a repair rule cut it in half,
   keeping one leaf. On the new photos that happened to 29 of 42, and 8 of them had a strip
   fraction of 0.4-0.5 - an even split, i.e. a spread being cut down the middle.
   Now: a rule may only remove a sliver smaller than a quarter of the page. Both leaves stay.
   This follows your rule: both pages visible and centred means one scan; a single page only
   when the other leaf is barely visible.

2. A QUAD WITH A LOST CORNER IS REJECTED
   Before: a corner pulled deep inside the page (the detection model's peak snapping onto a
   text block) left a quad that was nearly a triangle, and it passed every shape check.
   Now: a quad with an interior angle wider than 125 degrees is refused, and the fallback
   detector takes over. Measured: the four broken quads sat at 127-133 degrees; every good
   quad on the 116-photo set is at or below 123.

3. THE QUAD-TRIMMING RULE IS SWITCHED OFF
   There was a rule that trimmed a strip off one side of a detected quad, meant to cut away
   an absorbed facing page. Measured against the same photos with and without it, it damaged
   36 of the 38 photos it touched: on the new set it made 20 of 20 touched quads worse. It
   was locking the edge of the printed text block and throwing away a few centimetres of
   paper. Example: on one photo the page ends at x=1064, the detector before trimming says
   x=1148, and the trimming rule cut at x=1000 - 64 px into the paper.
   It is off. If it ever proves useful the switch is MLSD_REPAIR_ENABLED in the detector.

MEASURED RESULT

  42 new photos (never used for tuning, scored against a segmented page mask):
       quad locks the page      7 / 42  (17 %)   ->   23 / 42  (55 %)
       mean overlap (IoU)       0.637            ->   0.756
       at a looser threshold    24 / 42  (57 %)   (IoU >= 0.80)
       at a stricter one        18 / 42  (43 %)   (IoU >= 0.90)
       24 photos improved, 3 got worse, 15 unchanged.

  116 original photos (same scoring oracle as before, so this is a pure detector comparison):
       quad is the page         44 / 94  (47 %)   ->   54 / 94  (57 %)
       mean overlap (IoU)       0.481            ->   0.536
       detections               104              ->   104   (unchanged)

  The 3 photos that got worse are the broken quads described in fix 2: the broken quad is
  refused, and the fallback detector returns a different, smaller rectangle.

WHAT IS STILL WRONG

  The remaining failures are mostly the detector stopping short of the page edge, or locking
  a panel printed inside a page (a white sticker sheet lying on a book cover). 11 of 42 new
  photos still fall short and 5 are partially wrong. That is corner-accuracy work, not page
  choice, and it is the next thing to fix.

  These results come from an emulator replaying photos through the real detector, not from
  a physical phone camera.

INSTALL

RELEASE APK  (signed, recommended)
  1. If a DEBUG build is already installed, remove it first:
       adb uninstall com.docscanai
  2. adb install -r DocScanAI-0.3.17.apk
  3. No PC? Copy DocScanAI-0.3.17.apk to the phone and open it from the file manager.

DEBUG APK  (for bug reports; different signing key)
  adb install -r DocScanAI-0.3.17-debug.apk

Both are arm64-v8a. Android 8.0+ (API 26). No Play services needed.
The AI models download in-app on first launch, or are pushed by tooling.
